ACCESSIBILITY

A museum open to all.

How we make this site usable by everyone, what is still missing, and how to tell us about a problem.

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to visit the museum online. This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the standard referred to by European norm EN 301 549 and Italian accessibility law (Law 4/2004).

Compliance status

The site is partially compliant. This statement is based on a self-assessment carried out on 16 November 2026.

What we have done

  • Text alternatives for photos and logos.
  • Pages readable on phones without sideways scrolling, and zoom allowed.
  • Links and buttons at least 44 pixels high, with a visible focus outline for keyboard users.
  • The before-and-after photo sliders work with the keyboard (arrow keys) as well as touch and mouse.
  • Clear headings, a menu on every page and an Italian | English switch at the top.

Known limits

  • The film is on YouTube and has no captions or transcript yet.
  • Some small grey or gold text may not reach full contrast on every background.
  • Some descriptions of the artefacts are still being written.

We are working on these points.

Feedback and contact

If you find a barrier or need any content in another format, write to info@museumofagriculturalcivilization.org. We will reply as soon as we can, and in any case within 30 days.

Last update

Statement prepared on 16 November 2026. It will be reviewed at least once a year and after any major change to the site.
