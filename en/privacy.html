Your privacy.

This site sets no cookies and does not track you. Here is what happens to your data.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Carlo Montesanti, Museum of Agricultural Civilization, Contrada Piano Milo, Noto (SR), Sicily, Italy. For any question about your data, write to info@museumofagriculturalcivilization.org.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • It has no forms, no accounts and no newsletter.
  • Fonts and all other files are served from this site, not from third parties.

Data processed when you visit

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver pages and keep the service secure, the host automatically records technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We do not use these data ourselves. GitHub processes them under its own privacy statement; transfers to the USA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. GitHub privacy statement.

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and what you write only to reply to you. The legal basis is your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering (Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep the messages for as long as needed to deal with your request, and in any case no longer than 24 months, unless the law requires otherwise. We do not share them with anyone, except our email provider, which stores the mailbox.

Links to other sites

The film opens on YouTube, and other links may lead to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy and cookie policies; this site sends them no data until you click.

Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to info@museumofagriculturalcivilization.org. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

Changes

If the site starts using cookies or new services, this page will be updated before they are switched on. Last updated: 16 November 2026.
